01 — THE OPPORTUNITY

FLEET DUTY IS
NOT A TEST TRACK.

Electric vehicles are often designed around predictable conditions. Many working fleets operate in environments that place very different demands on a vehicle.

01 / COMMON DESIGN ASSUMPTIONS

Designed for the predictable.

  • Paved, predictable roads
  • Moderate climate
  • Reliable charging access
  • Light, mostly urban use
02 / FLEET OPERATING REALITIES

Built around demanding duty.

  • Sustained vibration and shock
  • Rough, unpaved terrain
  • Dust and heat
  • Heavy daily utilisation

WHY IT MATTERS Fleets buy on uptime and total cost of ownership. Durability and service continuity are central considerations in the adoption of electric mobility. Blair Motors is developing an engineering-led vehicle platform shaped by these operating realities.
